Bradpitt

Brad Pitt played Tyler Durden in the movie Fight Club, one of the most iconic characters in modern cinema. Tyler is a charismatic, rebellious soap salesman who rejects consumerism and encourages people to break free from society's expectations. As the film progresses, he becomes the driving force behind an underground fight club and a larger anarchic movement. Brad Pitt's intense performance, distinctive style, and memorable dialogue made Tyler Durden a cultural icon.
